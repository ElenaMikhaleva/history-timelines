(01) WIKI INDEX KINEMATICS & DYNAMICS

Motion & Vector Diagram Conventions

Motion describes the continuous change in an object's position over time relative to a specific reference frame. Understanding kinematics and dynamics requires a firm grasp of scalar and vector quantities, mathematical equations, and standard diagram conventions.
(01)

Fundamental Quantities

When drawing vector diagrams, color conventions help maintain visual clarity between displacement, velocity, force, and energy vectors. Distinguish rates of change from interactions.

(01.A) POS & VEL Blue
Displacement (x) & Velocity (v)

Change in position and the rate of change of position over time.

(01.B) ACC & FORCE Red
Acceleration (a) & Force (F)

Rate of change of velocity and the interaction causing mass to accelerate.

(01.C) MOM & ENERGY Green
Momentum (p) & Energy (E)

Product of mass and velocity (p = mv) and the capacity to do work.
