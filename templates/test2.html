Geological Time Scale

A survey of deep time eons, terrestrial formation, and evolutionary milestones.

Earth's deep history is partitioned into eons defined by fundamental geochemical transformations, oceanic formation, and major evolutionary transitions across deep time.

4567 Ma — Origin ◆ Scale 1:1,000,000 Yrs Present Day — 0 Ma

Hadean Eon

4567 – 4031 Ma

ETMY: Greek Hades (underworld) + aiōn (life force)

Characterized by the initial accretion of terrestrial mass, severe planetary bombardment, mantle differentiation, and early ocean condensation.

Archean Eon

4031 – 2500 Ma

ETMY: Greek arkhē (origin) + aiōn (life force)

Cooling crust allows stable continental cratons to form alongside primitive anaerobic life forms.

  • Atmosphere dominated by methane, ammonia, and heavy greenhouse gases
  • Emergence of initial prokaryotic microfossils and stromatolite reefs
  • Stabilization of early oceanic basins and volcanic arc systems

Proterozoic Eon

2500 – 538 Ma

ETMY: Greek protero- (former) + zōḗ (life)

Massive oxygenation of atmospheric reservoirs triggers oxygenation events, global glaciations, and complex eukaryotic evolution.

Phanerozoic Eon

538 – 0 Ma

ETMY: Greek phanerós (visible) + zōḗ (life)

Rapid diversification of complex multicellular life across Paleozoic, Mesozoic, and Cenozoic eras, marked by hard-shelled fossils and terrestrial ecosystems.
