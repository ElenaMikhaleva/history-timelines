HISTORY PALEONTOLOGY

Geological Time

Hadean Eon

Hadean Eon Катархей

from Greek Hades (hell); aiōn - 'life force'

4567 – 4031 million years ago

From formation of Earth to the appearing of solid rocks.

During Hadean Eon Earth’s surface was unstable, the mantle brought molten rock to the surface.

Archean Eon

Archean Eon Архей

from Greek arkhē (origin); aiōn - 'life force'

4031 – 2500 million years ago

From appearing of solid rocks.

During Archean Eon

  • atmosphere contained methane, ammonia, and other gases that most life on Earth today would not be able to breathe;
  • rocks and the continents began to form at the beginning of this period;
  • primitive atmosphere and oceans emerge;
  • the first living organisms — bacteria — appeared.

Proterozoic Eon

Proterozoic Eon Протерозой

from Greek protero- (former) and ζωή - zōḗ (life); aiōn - 'life force'

2500 – 538 million years ago

During Proterozoic Eon

  • the continents began to stabilize and build up, though they looked quite different than they do today;
  • bacteria, fungi, simple plants, and complex organisms, including the first animals, evolved;
  • the atmosphere became enriched in oxygen.

Phanerozoic Eon

Phanerozoic Eon Фанерозой

from Greek φανερός - phanerós (visible) and ζωή - zōḗ (life); ; aiōn - 'life force'

538 – 0 million years ago

Named so because it was believed that life began in the Cambrian, due to the lack of Precambrian fossil record back then.

During Phanerozoic Eon appeared plants able to carry out the photosynthetic process and thus release free oxygen into the atmosphere.

Paleozoic Era

Paleozoic Era Палеозой

538 – 252 million years ago

The continents began to move around during the Paleozoic, and by the end of the era all of the continents had come together to form one giant continent Pangaea.

Periods:

  • Cambrian;
  • Ordovician;
  • Silurian;
  • Devonian;
  • Carboniferous;
  • Permian.

Mesozoic Era

Mesozoic Era Мезозой

252 – 66 million years ago

Periods:

  • Triassic;
  • Jurassic;
  • Cretaceous.

Cenozoic Era

Cenozoic Era Кайнозой

66 – 0 million years ago

Periods:

  • Paleogene;
  • Neogene;
  • Quaternary.
