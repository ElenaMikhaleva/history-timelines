During Phanerozoic Eon abundant animal and plant life has proliferated, diversified and colonized various niches on the Earth's surface.

Paleozoic Era Палеозой

538 – 252 Ma

Continents drifted and eventually collided into one supercontinent, Pangaea.

  • Cambrian: Cambrian explosion - a dramatic expansion of animal life in the sea. Animals are beginning to evolve shells and exoskeletons. The forms of some animals show ancestry to modern animals. Jawless fish, the first vertebrates, appear.
  • Ordovician: The seas are diverse and the first coral reefs emerge. Algae is the only multicellular plant, and there is still no complex life on land.
  • Silurian: A mass extinction took place, wiping out nearly half of marine invertebrate species. The first land plants emerge, starting at the edge of the ocean. Plants evolve vascularity, the ability to transport water and nutrients through their tissues. Ocean life becomes larger and more complex, and some creatures venture out of reefs and onto land.
