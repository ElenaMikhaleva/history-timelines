Carnivora

~55 Ma · common ancestor

The order that gave rise to nearly all of today's land predators. Early in its history it split into two suborders that took carnivory in different directions — one toward specialized hunting, the other toward flexible, often omnivorous diets.

Feliformia Catlike

Retractable claws and a diet that stays close to pure carnivory. Includes cats, hyenas, civets, and mongooses.

Lion

The only cat that lives and hunts in social prides.

Tiger

The largest living cat; a solitary ambush hunter.

Spotted Hyena

Looks dog-like, but is genetically a feliform.

Meerkat

A small, highly social mongoose relative.

Caniformia Doglike

Non-retractable claws and often flexible, omnivorous diets. Includes dogs, bears, weasels, and seals.

Gray Wolf

A pack hunter, and ancestor of the domestic dog.

Red Fox

The most widely distributed wild carnivoran on Earth.

Brown Bear

One of the largest living land carnivorans.

Raccoon

A dexterous, mostly nocturnal omnivore.
